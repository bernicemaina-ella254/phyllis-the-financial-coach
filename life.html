Different Insurance Products For The Safety of your Life

  1. Education policy - UsomiBora
  2. Whole Of Life
  3. Endowment Assurance with Profits

1.UsomiBora

Give your child the education they deserve. UsomiBora combines flexible savings monthly, quarterly, or yearly with life cover for total peace of mind.

2.Whole Of Life

Whole of Life Plan is designed to give you and your loved ones financial security for life. It pays a lump sum (one-time payment) to your beneficiaries when you pass away and also provides coverage in case of disability or a serious illness.

3.Endowment Assurance with Profits

Save for the future while protecting your family today. With this simple plan, your regular contributions grow with bonuses into a rewarding lump sum—and if the unexpected happens, your loved ones still receive the full guaranteed amount plus bonuses.

4.Investsure

Saving for land, a new car, or your first home deposit? Turn big goals into reality with the InvestSure Plan. Start from just KES 2,000 a month or a KES 50,000 lump sum—grow your money, earn returns at maturity, and keep your loved ones protected. Smarter saving starts here.

5.Summit Endowment Plan

Save for your car, property, or child’s education in 5, 6, or 7 years with the ICEA LION Summit Endowment Plan. It also protects you against serious illness, disability, or loss of life—so your family never has to sell your assets during tough times.
